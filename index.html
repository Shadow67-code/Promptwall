ARCHITECTURE & PURPOSE

Why PromptWall?

AI agents introduce a semantic attack surface: a message can be technically valid yet behaviorally dangerous.

Detection methodology

PromptWall combines message structure, contextual patterns, intent rules, protected-resource checks, role policy and behavioral anomaly signals. Each contribution is shown in its final decision.

Prototype boundaries

This cybersecurity research demo is not a production control or a guarantee of detection accuracy. Production systems require validated models, identity controls, secure logging and human review.
